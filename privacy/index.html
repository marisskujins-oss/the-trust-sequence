Privacy Policy

How thetrustsequence.com and its associated LinkedIn integration handle your data.

Last updated: 25 April 2026

Who this covers

This policy applies to thetrustsequence.com, operated by Maris Skujins, and to the LinkedIn integration application registered under the same name. The integration exists to publish posts authored by Maris Skujins to his own personal LinkedIn profile on a schedule. It is not used to access, post on behalf of, or collect data from any other person.

Information we collect

From visitors to thetrustsequence.com

If you submit your email address through the early-access form, we store that email address for the sole purpose of notifying you when the book becomes available. We do not sell, share, or use your email for any other purpose. The site uses no analytics trackers, no third-party advertising scripts, and sets no marketing cookies.

From the LinkedIn integration

The LinkedIn integration authenticates as Maris Skujins through LinkedIn's standard OAuth 2.0 flow. It stores:

  • An access token and refresh token issued by LinkedIn, used to publish posts
  • Basic profile information (name, profile URL) returned by LinkedIn during sign-in
  • The text and image content of posts that are queued for publishing, plus a log of posts that have been published (including post URL and timestamp)

These tokens authorise the application to act only on behalf of Maris Skujins, only on his own LinkedIn profile. The application does not collect, request, or store information about any other LinkedIn user.

How information is used

  • Email addresses are used only to send book launch notifications.
  • OAuth tokens are used only to call LinkedIn's API to publish posts and add the first comment under each post.
  • Post content is published to Maris's LinkedIn profile and retained in a local log for operational purposes (so we can see what went out, when, and whether it succeeded).

How information is stored

OAuth tokens, queued posts, and the post log are stored on a private server controlled by Maris Skujins. Files are not world-readable and are not accessible from the public internet except through authenticated API endpoints. Tokens are kept only for as long as needed to operate the integration and are deleted on request.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any of the above information to third parties. The only outbound data transfers are to LinkedIn's own API, which is the destination platform for the posts.

Your rights

You can:

  • Request that your email address be removed from the early-access list at any time.
  • Revoke the LinkedIn integration's access at any time from your LinkedIn account settings under Data privacy → Other applications. Revocation immediately invalidates the stored tokens.

Children

The site and integration are not directed at children under 16 and do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the “Last updated” date above will reflect the change. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about data held about you: maris.skujins@gmail.com.
