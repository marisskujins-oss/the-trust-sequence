The Three Doors

Safety, then Context, then Decision. Skip one and the conversation breaks. If you're the one deciding, it doubles as a test: never say yes in a room where you couldn't say "I don't understand."

07 Photographs

Inside the grid, with a caption. Never full-bleed.

  • full-bleed images or images behind text
  • tinting the page to match the photo; its colours stay inside the frame
  • a photo without a caption and a credit
  • crops outside 3:2, 16:9 and 4:5
  • logos or watermarks left in; crop them out

08 Wordmark

The title, set in the system serif.

Clear space on every side equals the height of the capital T. Never smaller than 20 px tall on screen or 6 mm in print. Ink on paper; paper on ink in print only. Never on the photograph, never in the accent.

09 Forbidden

What makes a page read as generated.

  • dark grounds
  • the accent on a dark ground
  • centred layouts by default; centre only by decision
  • decorative italics
  • gradients
  • drop shadows
  • a second accent, or the accent used for decoration
  • em dashes in any copy
